How to use Turn the page

  1. Open a PDF from your computer.
  2. Start gestures and allow camera access.
  3. Swipe right to left in the air for the next page, left to right for the previous one.
  4. Hold a fist for about a second to pause. The camera turns off. Press G or click Start gestures to resume.

Tips: sit about an arm's length from the camera, keep your hand in good light, and make one quick, clear sweep across the camera. Bringing your hand back slowly afterwards won't turn the page back. Arrow keys (← →) work too.

Hand tracking runs in your browser. No video, images or books are uploaded.
