      How to use Turn the page

      1. Open a PDF from your computer.
      2. Start gestures and allow camera access.
      3. Swipe right to left in the air for the next page, left to right for the previous one.
      4. Swipe down to fold the camera window away, and swipe up to open it again. Hold your hand still for a moment first.
      5. Hold a fist for about a second to pause. The camera turns off. Press G or click Start gestures to resume.
      6. Close any time. Your place in each book is saved in this browser, and the next time you open that PDF it opens where you left off. Recent books reopen from the arrow next to Open PDF.

      Tips: sit about an arm's length from the camera, keep your hand in good light, and make one quick, clear sweep across the camera. Bringing your hand back slowly afterwards won't turn the page back. If the camera loses your hand, a note appears by the camera window saying why.

      Reading: zoom with + and − (0 fits the page again). When zoomed in, a swipe or arrow key scrolls down a screen first, then turns the page. To look around a zoomed page, pinch your thumb and index finger together to grab it, move your hand to drag, and open your fingers to let go. It settles on a whole page: a clear sideways drag moves to the page on that side, otherwise it keeps the page that fills most of the view. Click and drag with the mouse works too. F is full screen, C opens and closes the contents sidebar. Arrow keys (← →) turn pages too.

      Hand tracking runs in your browser. No video, images or books are uploaded.
